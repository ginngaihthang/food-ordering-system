# Staff guide

## Start of shift

1. Log in at `/admin/login`.
2. Open **Live orders** and press **Enable sound**. Browsers only allow sound after a click, so do this every time the page loads.
3. Keep the tablet plugged in with auto-lock turned off. A sleeping screen cannot ring.
4. Check the status. Green **Receiving orders** means you're live. A red **Connection lost** bar means the wifi dropped, and orders placed meanwhile appear when it reconnects.

## Dine-in

1. A new order rings and appears in **New** with its table number.
2. Press **Accept**, then **Ready** when the food is done, then **Hand over** (or **Mark served** in the bill) when it reaches the table.
3. Guests can order again at any time. Every order joins the same table bill.

## Takeaway

- Orders appear in **New** with a pickup number. Accept, Ready, Hand over.
- **If online payment is on:** an order only appears after the customer has paid, and its card shows **Paid online**.
- **If online payment is off:** take payment on **Takeaway orders** with the Cash or Online buttons, and print a receipt if asked.
- Print the counter poster from **Tables → Takeaway QR**.

## Taking payment for a table

1. **Tables → View bill** on the table.
2. Press **Mark served** on any order that is Ready.
3. Type the amount received, or tap **Exact** or a note button. Check **Change to give**.
4. Press **Confirm payment**, then print the receipt if asked.
5. Press **Close session**. This unlocks only when the bill is paid and every order is served.

## Cancelling an order

Press **Cancel** on the order card and confirm. The customer sees it as cancelled and it is removed from the bill. A cancelled order cannot be brought back, so ask the customer to order again. A table order cannot be cancelled after the bill is paid. An order paid online is refunded automatically.

## Sold-out dishes

**Products**, then switch **Available** off. The dish stays on the menu greyed out, and anyone who already has it in a cart is told.

## Admin only

Editing or deleting products, categories and tables, regenerating a table QR, Settings and Users.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No sound | Press **Enable sound** and check the device volume |
| A customer says their order is missing | Check the connection bar, then look in **Order history** for the day |
| A table won't close | The bill must be paid and every order served. The bill dialog shows which is missing |
| A customer's QR opens the wrong page | The QR was printed from a test address. Print it again from the live site |
| Forgot your password | Ask an admin to reset it under **Users** |
